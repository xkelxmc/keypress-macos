## License change

Show KeyPress was originally released under the MIT License. On 2026-07-28 the
repository moved to the PolyForm Strict License 1.0.0.

A license change is not retroactive, and it cannot revoke a license already
granted. Everything published up to and including commit `039d042` — which
covers every release through `v1.1.0-build.9` — was made available under the
MIT License. Anyone who obtains that code, **including by cloning this
repository today and checking out that commit or that tag**, receives the
rights the MIT License granted, and keeps them permanently. Those rights
include the right to use, modify, distribute, and sell copies of that version
of the code.

Everything committed after `039d042` is published under the PolyForm Strict
License 1.0.0 together with the Additional Permissions stated in
[LICENSE](./LICENSE). The app distributed through the Mac App Store is covered
by the Apple EULA — see Scope above.

The change was made because Show KeyPress is a paid product on the Mac App
Store. MIT expressly permits selling copies, which allowed anyone to republish
Show KeyPress as their own paid app. The source stays public so that anyone can
audit what an app holding Input Monitoring permission actually does with
keystrokes — reading the code, auditing it, and building it to check it for
yourself are permitted, whether or not you are a business.
