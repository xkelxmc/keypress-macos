# Notices

## License change

Keypress was originally released under the MIT License. On 2026-07-28 the
project moved to the [PolyForm Strict License 1.0.0](./LICENSE).

A license change is not retroactive. Everything published up to and including
commit `039d042` — which covers every release through `v1.1.0-build.9` — was
made available under the MIT License and remains available under those terms
forever. Anyone who obtained that code keeps the rights MIT granted them.

Everything committed after `039d042`, including all later releases, is licensed
under the PolyForm Strict License 1.0.0 only.

The change was made because Keypress is a paid product on the Mac App Store.
MIT expressly permits selling copies, which allowed anyone to republish Keypress
as their own paid app. The source stays public so that anyone can audit what an
app holding Input Monitoring permission actually does with keystrokes — reading
and auditing the code is explicitly permitted.
